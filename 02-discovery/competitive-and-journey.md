# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A loyal, long-time subscriber who opens the app most evenings expecting to watch something.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** To find something she actually wants to watch tonight without it feeling like work.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** She scrolls for about 20 minutes through 15,000 titles, closes the app without watching anything, and puts on a DVD instead.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** She puts on a DVD instead.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. She opens the app in the evening, expecting to watch something.
2. She scrolls through the home rows for about 20 minutes.
3. Nothing feels right, so she closes the app without starting anything.
4. She puts on a DVD instead.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Twenty minutes of the evening go to browsing, and none of that time ends in viewing.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** She went elsewhere within a single evening. The alternative isn't even another streaming service.
